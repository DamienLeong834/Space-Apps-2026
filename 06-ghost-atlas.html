▮ The Ghost Map Method

In 1854 John Snow plotted cholera deaths as stacked bars on a street map of London. The cluster — not any single case — pointed to one water pump. This atlas does the same with abandoned spacecraft: every correct answer is a bar on the zone where that hardware sits. Read enough bars and the physics of why these machines survive stops being a list of facts and becomes a pattern you can see.
