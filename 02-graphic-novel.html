Chapter One

Ninety days

Touchdown confirmed. Welcome to Meridiani Planatum.
JPL · Jan 25, 2004
We were built for 90 sols. Ninety days of driving, measuring, looking. Then we were supposed to go quiet.
VRRRM!

▮ Mission Fact File — Opportunity (MER-B)

The science behind the story
90 solsPlanned lifespan

Engineers designed it to work for three months. It kept operating for almost fifteen years.

45 kmTotal distance driven

A record for off-world driving that stood until NASA's Perseverance rover.

BlueberriesThe key discovery

Thousands of small grey hematite spheres covering the ground — they only form in liquid water.

Jun 2018The end

A planet-wide dust storm cut off sunlight. The battery drained and the rover fell silent.

Chapter Two

The stones that sang

Meridiani was a beach. Not a beach with water today — a beach where water used to be.
These spherules… they're hematite. They form in water. This whole plain was underwater.
Opportunity · Sol 33
Endurance Crater. We drove down into it — a rover that could never climb back out — because the rocks at the bottom were 100 million years older than the surface.
There is no air up here. No rain to wash me clean. When I stop, I stop forever.
Opportunity

▮ Science Fact File — Water on Mars

What these discoveries actually proved
HematiteMineral found

Grey "blueberries" littered the ground. Hematite crystallises from water — so Meridiani once held standing water.

Rock layersRead like pages

Sedimentary bands in Endurance Crater recorded repeated wetting and drying — a habitable chemistry.

SulfatesAcidic sea

The minerals pointed to a shallow, salty, somewhat acidic sea — not a gentle lake.

1,300+ solsSolar-panel survival

Dust storms repeatedly cleaned the panels by wind gusts, extending the mission far beyond plan.

Science summary based on NASA/JPL MER mission findings.
Chapter Three

It's getting dark

A global dust storm has engulfed Mars. Sunlight has dropped by more than 99%.
JPL · June 2018
The dust blocked the Sun for months. My panels could not charge. Each night I had a little less to give.
Opportunity · Sol 5111
My battery is low…
and it's getting dark.
Final message
…
Over 1,000 attempts to re-establish contact. No answer.
February 2019. NASA declared the mission over. Opportunity remains at Meridiani Planum — wheels on the ground, arm folded, facing the sky it could no longer see.
